Assign a Roster to a Teacher

As an admin, upload a CSV to add students to another teacher's roster — for teachers who aren't loading their own. The students are attached to the teacher you pick below, not to you.

Columns required: first_name, last_name, grade. Grade must be 7 through 12.

Example: 

first_name,last_name,grade
Jane,Smith,9
John,Doe,11

These students will be added to this teacher's roster.
